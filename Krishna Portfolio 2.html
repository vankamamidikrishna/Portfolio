About

A training & operations leader who builds programs from the ground up — and proves them with data.

V. Krishna Kumar is a results-driven Training & Operations professional with 15+ years of experience across skill development and employability programs, including 4+ years directly leading training teams and multi-centre operations. He currently leads cluster-level operations for Tech Mahindra Foundation's Digital Academy in Hyderabad — a CSR-funded skill development initiative — where he owns admissions, mobilization, and placements across two training centres serving underserved youth.

His remit spans the full operating cycle of a training organization: program implementation, session and training planning, budget and resource utilization, stakeholder and partner engagement, and MIS and performance reporting that keeps leadership decisions grounded in data. He builds trainer capacity through structured mentoring and Classroom Observations (CRO), using the resulting quality data to drive continuous improvement across centres.

Before moving into program and operations leadership, Krishna Kumar spent over a decade as a faculty leader and Head of Department in graphic design, UI, animation and multimedia education — instructing, assessing, and mentoring thousands of learners, and developing curriculum aligned to industry standards. That creative-education foundation now underpins his approach to training quality, employer engagement, and learner-centric program design.

Case 01

Multi-Centre Training Operations

Running one training centre well is an operations problem. Running two — with shared trainers, shared budget, and a single quality bar — is a coordination problem. This case study traces how Krishna Kumar structures cluster-level operations at Tech Mahindra Foundation's Digital Academy.

Challenge

Deliver consistent admissions, training quality, and placement outcomes across two CSR-funded centres serving underserved youth, without duplicating resources.

Approach

Centralize session planning and budget/resource allocation, while keeping trainer mentoring and classroom observation close to each centre's day-to-day delivery.

Execution

Coordinated 12 trainers across both centres, ran structured Classroom Observations, and managed ₹1.2 Cr in resource utilization aligned to program targets.

Outcome

450+ learners supported annually, with a 100% program completion rate and 100% placement outcome across the cluster.
